## Playtest fixes

- Keyboard shortcuts in `public/js/keys.js` (F, A, M, S, G, X, Tab, H, Esc) and right-click to move.
- Stacks form on any plot you own.
- A lost capital moves to the nearest plot still owned.
- Fine region maps at 0.05 degrees: Europe went from 700 by 380 to 1400 by 760 plots. Fine maps allow up to 100 bots.
- The route preview for short moves said "null plots and 0 s"; fixed.
- The gzipped maps and the art kit are committed, so a fresh clone deploys.

## Bugs found by testing, all fixed

- Combat only fought when the first stack was hostile to the second, not the other way round.
- A spawn could claim islands inside its radius that it could not walk to.
- A stack could form at a capital the nation no longer owned.
- Splitting a stack copied its route.
- Bots piled up over 9,000 idle stacks until they folded them back in.
- The coarse route graph missed 7.7 percent of routes until it used land regions.
