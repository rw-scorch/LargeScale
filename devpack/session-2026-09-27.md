## Bugs found by testing, all fixed

- Catch-up took 77 s for 12 hours until systems got whole-step versions and at most 240 steps.
- Long catch-up steps emptied towns until growth was damped per step and income paid after the town update.
- Closing a pocket mid-advance made a stack "capture" its own land, paying troops and reporting an attack on yourself.
- A research queue through an era advance stopped and waited on the era's upgrade count.
- Guard fold-back lost troops above the cap.
- The overtime shrink event never reached players until it was made public.
- Test flakes fixed: the swipe test read new stacks before they arrived on Europe.

## Decisions Ryan made

- The troop cap is land plus people. World creation is for admins only. A removed player's nation stays as it is.
- Milestone three: unit types inside stacks, one training building line, no counters, everyone sees the full mix. The Medieval machines, and ships that carry stacks.
- The interface: a click on another nation's land shows info, with Attack in the ring; a right-click always opens the ring; the guided start comes with the interface.
- Milestone four: towers and forts change combat; stop at the end of Gunpowder; Gunpowder before the economy while away.
- Guard means stacks and troops at home that watch enemy movement and go out to intercept. The town hall and parliament gather.
- Overtime is a shrinking border; the host can schedule every event, and everyone sees all settings and info; the winner is the last player standing.
- Later, at his word: a new main menu, military as individual units, the Industrial slice and the dev panel after his checks.
